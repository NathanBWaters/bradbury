Key Context & Why It Matters

Stand before the ornate 1894 Queen Anne Victorian at 1712 Fillmore Street between Post andutter streets (37.7854° N, 122.4329° W)—San Francisco City Landmark #241—and you are looking at the physical heart of the postwar 'Harlem of the West.' Originally built at 1690 Post Street and rolled around the corner onto Fillmore Street in 1979 to escape the bulldozers of the San Francisco Redevelopment Agency, this building housed Charles 'Jimbo' Edwards's legendary Waffle Shop and backroom after-hours jazz sanctuary, **Jimbo's Bop City**, from 1950 to 1965—where Billie Holiday, Charlie Parker, Miles Davis, John Coltrane, Ella Fitzgerald, Duke Ellington, and Sammy Davis Jr. jammed from 2:00 a.m. until dawn—before serving for nearly four decades as the home of **Marcus Books**, the nation's oldest independent Black bookstore.
